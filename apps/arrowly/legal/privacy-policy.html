Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

Introduction

Arrowly is a single-player puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains what the game does and does not collect.

The short version: the game keeps nothing about you on our servers, because it has no servers. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK collects in order to serve ads, and what Apple processes if you make a purchase.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

None. Arrowly has no account, no login, and no text entry. There is nothing to sign up for.

Stored On Your Device

  • Progress: Which levels you have solved, your best result on each, and which level you are up to. This is a small file on your device. It never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.
  • Settings: Sound on/off, chosen language, and whether you own the Supporter Pack.

Collected By Third Parties

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): The free version displays a banner and occasional full-screen ads, and offers an optional ad you can watch for a hint. Google's advertising SDK may collect device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure those ads. This happens inside Google's SDK; we do not receive or store any of it. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases (Apple): The Supporter Pack is processed by the App Store. We never see your payment details — the app only receives a yes/no answer about whether the purchase is valid.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not ask for or access your camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, or calendar.
  • We do not request location permission. (Ad networks may infer approximate location from your IP address, as described above.)
  • We do not ask for permission to track you, and the app does not use Apple's advertising identifier for tracking.
  • We run no servers for this game and receive no analytics from it.
  • We do not know who you are.

How Information Is Used

  • Your progress file exists so the game remembers where you were.
  • Ad data is used by Google to select and measure the ads shown to you.
  • Purchase verification is used to remove ads and unlock unlimited hints.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads: You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there.
  • Remove ads entirely: The optional Supporter Pack removes all advertising.
  • Delete your data: Deleting the app removes the progress file and every setting. There is nothing left on our side to delete.

Data Security

Progress is stored in the app's private container, protected by iOS file protection. Because we hold no copy of it, there is no server-side store of your data to breach.

Children's Privacy

Arrowly contains no violence, no chat, and no user-generated content, and is rated for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The free version does display advertising; parents who prefer an ad-free experience can purchase the Supporter Pack.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
