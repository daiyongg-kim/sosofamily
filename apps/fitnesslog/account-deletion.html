Delete Your Account

FitnessLog - Workout Tracker — published by SoSo Family

Last updated: September 2026

You can delete your FitnessLog account and everything synced to our server at any time. You do not need to contact us first — the app can do it for you.

Delete it from the app

  1. Open FitnessLog.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and the workouts synced to our server are deleted, and you are signed out.

If you no longer have the app

Email sosofamily.ca@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, and ask us to delete your FitnessLog account. We use the sending address to find the account, so a request from a different address cannot be actioned.

We reply within 7 days and delete the account within 30 days of confirming it is yours.

What gets deleted

Everything we hold that belongs to you:

  • Your account: the email address and name we received from Google when you signed in, and your user ID.
  • Everything synced: workouts, sets, exercises, routines and app settings stored on our server.

What is not deleted, and why

  • Workouts saved only on your phone. Signing in is optional in FitnessLog, and anything you recorded without syncing never reached our server, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove it, delete the app or clear its data in your phone's settings.
  • Purchase records. If you bought Remove Ads, Google Play keeps that receipt, not us. Deleting your FitnessLog account does not cancel or refund it, and the purchase stays tied to your Google account.

How long it takes

  • In the app: your rows are removed from our database immediately.
  • Automated backups: our database provider keeps rolling encrypted backups. Copies in those backups are overwritten on their normal schedule and are gone within 30 days. We do not restore deleted accounts from them.
  • After that we keep nothing about you.

You may not need to delete anything

If you only want to stop syncing, sign out in the Profile tab instead. The app keeps working without an account — logging, routines and history all run on the phone. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect.
