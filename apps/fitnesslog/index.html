FitnessLog

Open it, log your sets, get back to training.

FitnessLog is a plain workout log: exercises, sets, reps and weight, a calendar of what you did, and the stats that show you're progressing. No account needed and no subscription.

Download on the App Store

What it looks like

Who it's for

People who lift or follow a structured program and just want a record of sets, reps and weight — without a sign-up wall or a monthly fee.

How it works

  1. Start a workout and add exercises from the built-in library of 129, or create your own.
  2. Log each set: weight and reps.
  3. Save sessions you repeat as routines and start the next workout from one.
  4. Check the calendar and the monthly stats — total volume, set counts, most-trained muscle groups and personal records.
