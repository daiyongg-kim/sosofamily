Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2026

Introduction

Monette is an expense tracker and household ledger for iPhone and Android. The two apps work the same way; differences on Android are described in Monette on Android. This Privacy Policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

The short version: your financial records never leave your phone. Monette has no account, no login, and no servers. The only data that leaves your device is what Google's advertising SDK collects in order to serve ads.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

None that reaches us. Monette has no sign-up, no login, and no cloud sync. We never receive your transactions, your balances, or anything else you enter.

Stored On Your Device

  • Transactions: amounts, dates, categories, accounts, notes and descriptions you enter. Stored in a database in the app's private container on your device.
  • Accounts and budgets: the cash, bank and card accounts you set up, their balances, and any category budgets.
  • Receipt photos: images you attach to a transaction, kept in the app's private container.
  • Settings: your display currency, monthly start date, categories and repeat rules.

All of this is removed when you delete the app.

Collected By Third Parties

  • Advertising (Google AdMob): Monette shows a banner along the bottom of the screen and one full-screen ad per day after a transaction is saved. Google's advertising SDK may collect device identifiers, coarse location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data in order to serve and measure those ads. This happens inside Google's SDK; we do not receive or store any of it. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Your transactions, amounts, notes and receipt photos are never sent to the ad network. The advertising SDK has no access to your financial data.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not have an account system, so we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We do not connect to your bank. Monette has no bank linking and never asks for banking credentials.
  • We do not request location permission. (Ad networks may infer approximate location from your IP address, as described above.)
  • We do not ask for permission to track you, and the app does not use Apple's advertising identifier for tracking.
  • We run no servers for this app and receive no analytics from it.
  • We do not know who you are.

Permissions

  • Camera: only used if you choose to photograph a receipt to attach to a transaction. The photo stays on your device.
  • Photo Library: only used if you choose to attach an existing image to a transaction.

Both are optional. Monette works fully without granting either.

How Information Is Used

  • Your transactions exist so the app can show your balances, budgets and statistics.
  • Ad data is used by Google to select and measure the ads shown to you.

Your Choices

  • Personalized ads: on iPhone, you can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, and reset your advertising identifier there. On Android, see Monette on Android.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes every transaction, account, receipt photo and setting. There is nothing left on our side to delete, because there never was anything.

Data Security

Your records are stored in the app's private container, protected by iOS file protection. Because we hold no copy of them, there is no server-side store of your financial data to breach.

Monette on Android

The Android app keeps your financial records on your phone just like the iPhone app. These points are specific to Android:

  • Storage and backups: your transactions, accounts, budgets, receipt photos and settings are stored in the App's private storage. Monette has no account or export, so they are included in Android's own backup to your Google account and in device-to-device transfer, so a new phone can restore them. Cloud backup is used only when your phone can encrypt it end to end (a screen lock is set). We never receive this backup. You can turn backup off in Android Settings.
  • Receipt photos: chosen with Android's photo picker, which gives the App only the photo you pick. Monette asks for no camera or storage permission. A copy is saved in the App's private storage with location metadata removed.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): the free version shows a banner on the main tabs and at most one full-screen ad a day, after you save a transaction. No banner is shown on the screens where you type amounts. Google's advertising SDK uses your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, and the ads may be personalized. Your transactions, amounts, notes and photos are never sent to the ad network.
  • Consent: where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form appears before any ad is requested. You can change your choice any time in More › Ad privacy choices.
  • Your ad choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (wording varies by phone). See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we receive only confirmation that the purchase exists, which the App checks to hide ads.

Children's Privacy

Monette is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does display advertising.
