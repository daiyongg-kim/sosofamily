Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Introduction

Pagevox is an EPUB and PDF reader for iPhone and Android that can read your books aloud. This policy explains what happens to your data. Differences on Android are described in Pagevox on Android. The short version: your books stay on your device, and we never see them.

Your Books Never Leave Your Device

Books you add to Pagevox are stored in the app's own folder on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. We operate no server that receives your files, and reading aloud happens entirely on the device using Apple's built-in speech system.

This also means your reading position, highlights and library are on your device only. If you delete the app, that data goes with it.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for and no password to forget.
  • No copies of your books, their contents, or anything the app reads aloud.
  • No name, email address, or contact details, unless you choose to email us.
  • No location data.

Advertising

The free version of Pagevox shows a single banner advertisement on the Library screen. Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to select and measure ads. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

Advertisements never appear while you are reading or listening.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you will be asked for your advertising choices before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from within the app.

A one-time Remove Ads purchase removes the banner permanently.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the app can unlock, but we never receive your payment details.

Permissions

Pagevox asks for access only to the files you choose to import. It does not request access to your photos, camera, contacts, microphone, or location.

Pagevox on Android

  • Storage: books, covers, reading progress, bookmarks and highlights are kept in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Backups: Android's backup to your Google account includes only your Pagevox settings (reading speed, voices, pronunciation rules, appearance), and only when your phone can encrypt it end to end. Books are never included. Moving to a new phone with Android's device-to-device transfer copies your whole library. We never receive either.
  • Reading aloud: uses your phone's text-to-speech engine with voices that work offline. Voices that send text to an online service are not used, so the text being read does not leave your phone.
  • Free Classics: the catalog of public-domain books, their covers and downloads come from Project Gutenberg (www.gutenberg.org). When you browse or search it, your search terms and your device's IP address reach Project Gutenberg's servers, under their privacy policy. This is the only server the app itself contacts.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): one banner on the Library screen only — never while you read or listen. Google's advertising SDK uses your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interaction and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads, and the ads may be personalized. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google's consent form appears first; you can change your choice in the app's settings. No book titles or contents are sent to the ad network. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads (wording varies by phone).
  • Remove Ads: a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; the app only checks that the purchase exists.
  • Permissions: internet (Free Classics, ads), a media-playback service and, on Android 13+, notifications so playback controls appear on the lock screen. No storage, camera, contacts, microphone or location permission — books are opened with Android's file picker or "Open with".

Children's Privacy

Pagevox is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does display advertising.
