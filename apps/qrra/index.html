Qrra

Make the code you need. Find it again later.

Qrra scans QR codes and barcodes, makes QR codes for your Wi‑Fi, a link or your contact card, and keeps everything you scanned or made in one place. No account — codes are made on your device.

Where it helps

Café or event Wi‑Fi

Make one Wi‑Fi code and print it at the counter. Guests scan it and join — no spelling out the password.

A menu or sign-up link

Turn the web address of your menu, booking form or RSVP page into a code for a table card or flyer.

Your contact card

Put your name, number and email in a code so people can save you to their contacts in one scan.

What it does

  1. Scan QR codes and common barcodes (EAN, UPC, Code 128, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec and more) with the camera, or from a photo on iPhone.
  2. Make QR codes for a phone number, website link, email, text message, contact card, plain text, Wi‑Fi network or app store link — plus Facebook, Instagram, X, WhatsApp and YouTube profiles.
  3. Keep what you scanned and what you made in history, with search and favourites.
  4. Share a code or save it as an image — and on iPhone, export it as a PDF to print.

Good to know: a regular QR code stores its content directly. Once it's printed you can't change where it points, and it doesn't count scans. If the link or Wi‑Fi password changes, make a new code.
