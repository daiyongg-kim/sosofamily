Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Qrra ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our QR code scanner and generator application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • QR Code Content: When you scan or create QR codes, the content is stored locally on your device for your convenience in the History feature.
  • Contact Information: If you create QR codes containing contact information (vCard), this data is processed locally and stored only on your device.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We may collect device-specific information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and analytics.
  • Camera Access: The App requires camera access to scan QR codes. Camera data is processed in real-time and is not stored or transmitted.
  • Photo Library Access: If you choose to import QR codes from your photo library or save generated QR codes, we request access to your photo library. This access is used solely for these features.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information unless you voluntarily provide it through QR code content.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, or other personal data beyond what is necessary for the App's core functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Improve, personalize, and expand the App
  • Understand and analyze how you use the App
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality
  • Display your scan history and created codes for your convenience

Data Storage

Local Storage

All QR code data, including scan history and created codes, is stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

Cloud Services

If you use subscription features, your subscription status may be verified through Apple App Store or Google Play Store services. We do not store your payment information.

Third-Party Services

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to help understand App usage. These services may collect information about your use of the App.

Advertising

The free version of the App may display advertisements. Third-party ad networks may collect information to provide personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings.

In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed through Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play Store (Android). Please refer to their respective privacy policies for information on how they handle your data.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (you can delete your scan history directly within the App)
  • Opt-out: Opt out of personalized advertising through device settings

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
