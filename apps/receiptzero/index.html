ReceiptZero

Receipts in, tax-ready report out.

ReceiptZero scans your receipts, reads the vendor, date and total on your phone, and exports a PDF or CSV you can hand to your accountant or keep for the CRA or IRS. Free to download, on iPhone and Android.

What it looks like

Screens from the iPhone app.

Who it's for

Freelancers, self-employed people, gig workers and anyone who travels for work and has to keep receipts for taxes or expense claims — in the US or Canada.

How it works

  1. Point the camera at a receipt. Text recognition runs on the phone and fills in the vendor, date and total.
  2. Check the details and put the receipt in an expense category.
  3. When it's time to file, pick a period and export a PDF summary or a CSV.
  4. Send the file to your accountant or keep it with your tax records.
