Scanory

Paper in, clean PDF out.

Scanory turns receipts, contracts, notes and forms into PDFs on your iPhone and keeps them sorted in folders. Free, with no watermark and no page limit.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it looks like

Who it's for

Anyone who needs a paper document as a PDF without paying for a subscription — students sending assignments, freelancers keeping signed contracts, anyone filing receipts or forms.

How it works

  1. Tap the scan button. The camera finds the page edges for you.
  2. Scan as many pages as the document has — they become one PDF, sized to A4 or B5.
  3. Rename it, put it in a folder, and find it later with search.
  4. Share the PDF by email, Messages, Files or any app on your iPhone.
