Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 21, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Scanory ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our document scanner application ("App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Scanned Documents: When you scan documents using the camera, the scanned images and generated PDF files are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or access the content of your scanned documents.
  • File Organization: Folders, file names, and organizational data you create within the App are stored locally on your device.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: We may collect device-specific information such as device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers for app functionality and analytics.
  • Camera Access: The App requires camera access to scan documents. Camera data is processed locally on your device and is not stored or transmitted to any server.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect personal identification information.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, or other personal data beyond what is necessary for the App's core functionality.
  • We do not upload or transmit your scanned documents to any external server.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App
  • Improve, personalize, and expand the App
  • Understand and analyze how you use the App
  • Develop new products, services, features, and functionality

Data Storage

Local Storage

All scanned documents, generated PDF files, folders, and related data are stored locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party servers. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time within the App.

Third-Party Services

Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect certain information to serve ads, including device identifiers and usage data. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings. For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

Analytics

We may use third-party analytics services to help understand App usage. These services may collect information about your use of the App.

Device Permissions

  • Camera: Required to scan documents. Camera data is processed locally and is not transmitted externally.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information. Since all document data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data also depends on your device's security settings. We recommend using device-level security features such as passcodes and biometric authentication.

Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (you can delete your scanned documents and files directly within the App)
  • Opt-out: Opt out of personalized advertising through device settings
