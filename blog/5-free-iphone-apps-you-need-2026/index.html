The pattern is always the same. Download, try, hit a wall, pay or delete.

These five apps break that pattern. They're fully free — no subscriptions, no ads, no feature gates. They're built by SoSo Family, a small team with a simple idea: everyday tools should just work, without charging you monthly for the privilege.

Here's what each one does, and why it's worth the space on your phone.

1. VoiceNote+ — Voice Transcription That's Actually Free

What it does: Records audio and converts it to text using AI — supporting 13 languages.

Record, Transcribe, Done. In 13 Languages.
Two AI engines. Zero subscriptions. Zero limits.

Most voice transcription apps charge for anything useful. Otter.ai gives you 300 minutes per month with a 30-minute cap per conversation on its free plan — then it's $8.33/month (see our detailed VoiceNote+ vs Otter.ai comparison). Rev is a paid service entirely. Apple's built-in voice memos don't transcribe at all.

VoiceNote+ gives you two transcription engines:

  • Apple Online — fast, real-time transcription when you have internet
  • OpenAI Whisper — offline, on-device processing with near-perfect accuracy (Large v3 model achieves ~97.9% accuracy)

That Whisper option is the standout. Your audio never leaves your device. No server, no account, no data collection. You download the model once, and transcription works anywhere — airplane mode, remote cabin, underground parking lot. Learn more about how offline Whisper transcription works on iPhone and which Whisper model is right for you.

Best for: Students recording lectures, professionals in free meeting transcription, journalists doing interview transcription, anyone who wants a free voice memo app without paying monthly.

2. FitnessLog — A Workout Tracker Without the Paywall

What it does: Logs sets, reps, and weight for any exercise. Tracks your progress over time.

Track Every Workout. See Every Rep.
Unlimited routines. No subscription. No caps.

The gym app market has a specific problem: nearly every popular tracker limits the free version in ways that make it useless for serious training.

  • Strong caps you at 3 routines. If you run a 4-day split, you've already exceeded the limit.
  • Hevy locks analytics and custom exercises behind $9.99/month.
  • JEFIT runs ads during your workout and charges $12.99/month to remove them.

FitnessLog gives you unlimited routines, full set/rep/weight logging, and progress tracking — all free, no ads. You open it, log your workout, and close it. That's it. See how it stacks up in our best free workout tracker comparison.

Best for: Anyone who lifts weights, does bodyweight training, or follows any structured exercise program and wants to track progress without managing a subscription.

3. Scanory — Document Scanner Without Watermarks

What it does: Scans documents with your camera and saves them as organized PDFs.

Scan to PDF. Organize in Folders.
No watermarks. No page limits. No subscription.

Document scanning apps are notorious for bait-and-switch pricing:

  • CamScanner stamps watermarks on your free-tier scans — and had a well-publicized privacy incident in the past.
  • Adobe Scan limits OCR to 5 pages on the free plan, then charges $9.99/month.
  • Apple Notes can scan, but organizing and managing PDFs is minimal at best.

Scanory scans documents cleanly, saves them as PDFs, and lets you organize everything into folders. No watermarks on any scan. No page limits. No subscription tier waiting to charge you. Read our best free document scanner comparison for a deeper look.

Best for: Students scanning lecture notes and handouts, freelancers digitizing receipts and invoices, anyone who needs to turn paper into PDF without paying Adobe prices.

4. Qrra — QR Codes, Both Ways

What it does: Scans QR codes and creates custom ones — with colors, logos, and design options.

Scan and Create QR Codes. Customize Them.
No ads. No limits. No premium tier.

Your iPhone camera can scan QR codes. What it can't do is create them. And if you need a custom QR code for your business, event, or personal use, most apps charge for generation or lock design features behind a subscription.

  • QR Reader shows ads during scanning and charges for the premium experience.
  • Scan Me limits creation features on the free tier.
  • iPhone Camera scans only — no generation, no customization.

Qrra handles both scanning and creating. Generate QR codes for URLs, contact cards, Wi-Fi credentials, or plain text. Customize colors and add logos. No ads interrupt the experience. Check out our complete QR code scanner and generator guide for more details.

Best for: Small business owners creating QR codes for menus or promotions, event organizers, marketers, or anyone who needs to generate and share QR codes without paying for a subscription.

5. SnapTip — The Bill Splitter That Does More

What it does: Scans receipts, calculates tips, and splits costs between any number of people.

Scan the Receipt. Calculate the Tip. Split the Bill.
All in one tap. All free.

Tipping and bill-splitting apps are a small category, but the existing options still manage to disappoint:

  • Tippy charges $0.99 upfront — not much, but it's the principle when the app does one simple calculation.
  • Calculator+ is a general calculator with tip features bolted on as an afterthought.
  • Mental math works until there are 8 people, 3 shared appetizers, and someone who didn't drink.

SnapTip combines receipt scanning with tip calculation and bill splitting. Point your camera at the receipt, set the tip percentage, enter the number of people, and you're done. No manual entry, no mental gymnastics. See our guide to splitting bills fairly on iPhone for tips and tricks.

Best for: Anyone who eats out with friends, splits household bills, or just wants a fast way to calculate tips without fumbling with a calculator app.

Download SnapTip

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these apps really free?

Yes — completely free. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no premium tiers. Every feature is available from the moment you download. SoSo Family doesn't gate functionality behind paywalls.

Do these apps have ads?

No. All five apps are ad-free. You won't see banners, pop-ups, or video ads at any point. The experience is clean from start to finish.

Are these apps safe for my privacy?

Absolutely. None of the apps require you to create an account, and your data stays on your device. VoiceNote+'s Whisper engine processes audio entirely on-device — nothing is sent to a server. All five apps are rated Ages 4+ on the App Store.

Do I need to create an account to use them?

No. You can open any of these apps and start using them immediately. There's no sign-up, no email verification, and no login screen. Just download and go.

What iPhone do I need?

All five apps work on any iPhone running a recent version of iOS. If your iPhone supports iOS 16 or later, you're good. For VoiceNote+'s larger Whisper models, a newer device (iPhone 12 or later) is recommended for faster processing.
