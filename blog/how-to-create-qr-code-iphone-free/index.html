How to Create a QR Code on iPhone for Free in 2026 (Step by Step)

Create a QR code on iPhone for free in 2026 — for a link, Wi-Fi, contact card, email, phone, SMS or text. Step-by-step with a free app, plus what a plain QR code can't do.

How to Create a QR Code on iPhone for Free in 2026 (Step by Step)

You can scan a QR code with your iPhone's camera. But the moment you try to make one — for your menu link, your Wi-Fi, your contact card, your event sign-up — the camera can't help.

Here's a straightforward way to create one on iPhone in about a minute, and what to know before you print it.

What iPhone Can Already Do

The iPhone camera (and the Control Center code scanner) reads QR codes natively. Recent iOS versions can also show a QR code for sharing a Wi-Fi network from the Passwords app, and Apple Shortcuts can generate a basic QR code from text or a link.

What iOS doesn't give you is one place to create different kinds of codes — a contact card, an email with a pre-filled subject, a phone number, a text message — save them, and find them again later. For that, you need an app.

What to Look For in a QR Generator

  • Free to create — not "free to scan, paid to generate"
  • The content types you need — link, Wi-Fi, contact, email, phone, SMS, text, social profiles
  • No watermark on the saved QR image
  • No subscription for the basics
  • A history so you can find a code again without recreating it

Before You Print: What a Plain QR Code Can't Do

Qrra makes static QR codes — the link or text is written into the pattern itself.

  • You can't change it after printing. If the menu link changes, you make a new code. (Tip: point the code at a page you control, like your menu page, and update that page instead.)
  • No scan statistics. A static code doesn't report how many people scanned it.
  • Standard black-and-white look. Qrra doesn't add colors or logos. The upside: high contrast is what cameras read most reliably.

If you need editable links or scan counts, you need a "dynamic QR" service, which usually costs a monthly fee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I make a QR code on iPhone without an app?

Partly. Apple Shortcuts can generate a basic QR code from text or a link, and the Passwords app can show a QR code to share a Wi-Fi network. For contact, email, phone or SMS codes — and a history of the codes you made — an app is easier.

Is Qrra free to generate QR codes?

How do I make a Wi-Fi QR code on iPhone for free?

Install Qrra, tap Create, choose Wi-Fi, enter the network name (SSID), password and security type (usually WPA2), then save it. Phones that scan it can join without typing the password.

Can I add my logo or change the colors?

Not in Qrra — it creates standard black-and-white codes. If you need a branded design, you'll need a design-focused tool.

Will the QR code expire?

No. A static QR code is just a pattern that encodes your link or text. It keeps working as long as the destination works — but you can't change what it points to after printing.

Will the QR code work on Android?

Yes. QR codes are a universal standard. A code made with Qrra scans on Android or any device with a QR reader. Qrra is also on Google Play.
