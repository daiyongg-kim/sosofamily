Why You Need a Meeting Transcription App

Manual note-taking during meetings has real costs:

  • You miss key details. Studies show people retain only about 10-20% of what they hear in meetings. The rest disappears the moment you walk out.
  • You lose focus. When you're writing, you're not fully listening. That means you might miss the most important decision of the entire meeting.
  • Handwritten notes are hard to search. Good luck finding that one action item from three weeks ago buried in a notebook.

A transcription app records the audio and converts it to searchable text automatically. You stay present in the conversation, and every word is captured.

Tips for Better Transcription Results

To get the most accurate transcripts from VoiceNote+:

  • Minimize background noise. Find a quiet spot or place your phone closer to the speaker.
  • Speak clearly. Natural pace works best -- you don't need to slow down, but avoid mumbling.
  • Choose the right language. Select the primary language being spoken before you start recording.
  • Use an external mic for large rooms. For conference rooms or lecture halls, a clip-on or Bluetooth mic improves audio quality significantly.
